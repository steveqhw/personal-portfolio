Privacy / contact

Contact and privacy information

This is a static portfolio. It does not use analytics, advertising cookies, accounts or browser storage.

Contact form

The contact form sends the name, email address and message you choose to provide to Formspree so that the message can be delivered. A language value and an anti-spam honeypot field are submitted with the form.

Provide only the information needed for the conversation. Do not submit credentials, health data, employer secrets or other confidential or sensitive information.

Processor and retention

Formspree is an external service and processes form submissions under its own terms and privacy practices. Retention depends on that service and the account configuration; this site does not promise a separate retention period.

Read Formspree's privacy policy ↗.

External destinations

LinkedIn and GitHub links take you to third-party websites. No data is sent to those services by this site until you choose to follow a link; their own privacy policies then apply.

Questions or requests

Use the contact form with a minimal message or contact Stefano through LinkedIn. Employer identities, direct private contact details and the private CV are not published here.
